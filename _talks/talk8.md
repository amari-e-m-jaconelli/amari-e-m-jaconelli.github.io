It is a long-standing result from Tracy and Widom that the (log derivative of the) Fredholm determinant induced by the finite-N GUE kernel solves the Painlevé-IV equation.
In this talk, I will discuss various extensions to this result, notably in the finite temperature regime, which expands the problem to the operator-level. This broader class of kernels can also be related to wider integrable systems, such as the famous AKNS system.
