The Tracy-Widom distributions for the largest eigenvalue for the GSE is typically written as (square roots of) a Fredholm determinant with integral operators defined by a 2x2 matrix kernel. However, a separate derivation gives the same distribution as (square roots of) a Fredholm determinants with integral operators defined by scalar kernels. Until present, there was no rigorous proof for why these objects would be equal, nor an explanation of when such simplifications are possible. This week, I will go through how to prove this and explore how the result can be expanded for other ensembles.
