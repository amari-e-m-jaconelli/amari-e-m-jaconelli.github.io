Based on joint work with Thomas Bothner, we have been able to show equivalence of the Fredholm determinants of integral operators induced by two broad matrix kernel classes to determinants of operators defined by scalar kernels. These two classes, ‘additive Hankel’ and ‘truncated Weiner-Hopf’, admit a Riemann-Hilbert characterisation which allows for the evaluation of the large value asymptotics of their respective Fredholm determinants.
In this talk, I will establish this connection and illustrate the structure of the associated Riemann-Hilbert problems.
