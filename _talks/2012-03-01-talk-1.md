In the paper 'A Simple Approach to Global Regime of the Random Matrix Theory', Pastur detailed a generalised method for obtaining asymptotic results for moments of the eigenvalue counting measure of large random matrices. This method is now commonly referred to as the 'resolvent method'. In this talk, I will briefly introduce the ideas behind this technique, as well as its benefits, and demonstrate how it recovers the semicircle law for the GUE.
