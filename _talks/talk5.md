Based on joint work with Thomas Bothner, we have been able to show equivalence of the Fredholm determinants of integral operators induced by two broad matrix kernel classes to determinants of operators defined by scalar kernels. These two classes, 'additive Hankel' and 'truncated Weiner-Hopf', are the appropriate classes to study soft-edge and bulk eigenvalue statistics, respectively, of typical symplectic and orthogonal random matrix ensembles.
In this talk, I will show how we prove this equivalence and discuss our hopeful next steps.
